FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first top-up.

How fast will I receive my diamonds?

Most orders are delivered in under a minute after payment is confirmed. In rare cases, delivery can take up to 15 minutes during peak hours.

Do you need my Mobile Legends password?

No. We only need your User ID and Zone ID to send diamonds directly to your account. Never share your password with anyone.

Where can I find my User ID and Zone ID?

Open Mobile Legends and tap your avatar in the top-left corner. Your User ID and Zone ID are displayed under your nickname in the format 12345678 (1234).

Which payment methods do you accept?

QRIS, GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely by our payment partners.

What if my diamonds don't arrive?

Contact our support team with your order number and account ID. If an order can't be delivered, you'll receive a full refund.
